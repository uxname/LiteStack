# ADR NNNN: <the decision in one line>

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN | withdrawn

## Context

What was happening that forced a choice. Facts only — constraints, numbers, the thing that
broke. No reasoning yet, and no options.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense, one or two sentences. Written so that a reader can tell
whether a given piece of code obeys it.

## Alternatives

What else was on the table and why it lost. One line each. An ADR with no alternatives is
usually a rule, not a decision.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what we now have to pay for it. Include the
cost we knowingly accepted — that is the part a future reader needs most.
